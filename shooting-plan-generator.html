Export as Markdown is your save file: plain text you can edit in iA Writer and re-import here to pick up where you left off. Export for blog pours the spoken scripts into flowing Markdown — scene labels dropped, visual cues stripped, emphasis and links kept in place — ready to edit into a Ghost post. In the script you can use Markdown **bold**, *italic* and [text](links); these carry through to the blog draft. Square-bracket notes with no link, like [over iNat photo], are treated as visual cues: shown on the aide, but not counted toward runtime and not carried to the blog. Tick Additional scene for vertical-only extras — the hooks and tails you shoot for pulled-out shorts: they stay in the plan and on the shooting aide but are left out of both runtime totals, since you'll time those when you cut them in the editor. Close-ups are timed slightly slower. The exported HTML tracks shot/not-shot on the phone via that browser's storage — re-exporting after edits starts that tracking fresh.
