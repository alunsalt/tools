Garden Prompter

A memory aid, not a cold-read scroller. It highlights where you are, waits when you stop, and follows when you carry on.

Notes for the iPad
  • Position first. “Set up position” shows a dashed box and a reading line, with no microphone. Move the reading line and column until the first line sits just above or beside the lens.
  • Taps. Left third goes back a phrase, right third goes forward a phrase, middle pauses or resumes. If listening has dropped, tapping also restarts it.
  • Voice following uses Safari's built-in speech recognition. Dictation must be switched on (Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation), and you need to allow the microphone when asked. It sends audio to Apple, so it needs a connection.
  • Microphone. A lav or USB mic plugged into the iPad is picked up automatically. Connect it before pressing Start.
  • Start delay. After you press Start, a countdown gives you time to walk over and sit down. Nothing listens or scrolls until it reaches zero, and tapping the screen starts it early. Set it to 0 to start at once.
  • When it can't hear you the text stays where it is and the pill turns amber. It never runs on ahead of you.
  • Without Safari's toolbars: Share → Add to Home Screen. If speech won't start from the home-screen icon, use it from a normal Safari tab instead.
  • Keyboard (for testing on a computer): arrows move, space pauses, Esc exits.
