Cover Seam Trials

Mock home covers with Hooker green fixed at the top of the masthead and the bottom of the foot, and the week's colours in the lower masthead and the ticker bar. The seam is drawn the way cover-verb.js draws it now: wavy blocks blurred 6px, with an 88px strip blurred 24px laid over the join. Bands are at real height, so the blur looks as it will on screen. Colours are computed with the same OKLCH contrast clamp as the live code.

The worst case, four ways

Hooker green into rust: near-opposites on the colour wheel. The blur mixes them in the browser's RGB space, and the halfway point of a blue-green and a red-brown is a grey-brown. This row ignores the treatment switch and shows all four together.

Combinations by family

One family per week: the lower masthead is always dark enough for white text, and the ticker can be dark (white ink) or pale (dark ink). The amber notes say what to look for; the green ones are the controls I expect to be fine.
