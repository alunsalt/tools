Cover Seam Trials

Four colour families for the home cover, as the theme would draw them. Hooker green is fixed at the top of the masthead and in the foot. Each week takes one family for the lower masthead and the This Week bar, with the bar either dark (white text) or pale (dark text). The colours come from the same logic as version 2.8.0's cover-verb.js, with two changes proposed here: a new berries family, and autumn's pale end stretched from ochre towards straw.

Seams are drawn as built: the masthead keeps its blur, a pale bar meets the foot along a crisp wave, a dark bar meets it with a soft blur. The cards are smaller than the real cover, so the masthead blur looks a little stronger here than on screen.

Sample weeks

Each family shows four sample weeks: two with a dark bar, two with a pale one. "Draw new weeks" picks fresh colours from the same ranges, the way a new TWiB address would. Paste your real accent colour to see the Menu button as it is.
